# 📣 hclipper Community Outreach & Pitch Playbook

This document provides ready-to-use communication templates, pitches, and outreach strategies to share **hclipper** with church media teams, Christian creator networks, podcasters, educators, and open-source communities.

---

## 🎯 The Core Value Proposition

> **"Turn 1-hour sermons and podcasts into 6 viral vertical shorts every week — 100% free, local-first, zero cloud subscriptions."**

### Why Churches and Creators Care
1. **Zero Recurring SaaS Costs**: No more $29-$89/month Opus Clip or Descript subscriptions eating into small church or independent creator budgets.
2. **Total Data Privacy**: Videos and audio never leave the creator's machine. Ideal for sensitive counseling, private teaching, or unpublished footage.
3. **True Cadence Awareness**: Trims awkward dead pauses while preserving the natural 0.34s breathing rhythm of powerful preaching and emotional speaking.
4. **Broadcast Vocal Mastering**: 80Hz highpass vocal EQ, spectral noise removal, dynamic compression, and -14 LUFS loudness mastering ready for phone speakers.
5. **Multi-Account Automation**: Publish simultaneously to YouTube Shorts, TikTok (including international and proxy-guarded US accounts), and Instagram Reels without logging into each app manually.

## 💬 Template 4: Reddit Post (r/videography, r/ChurchTech, r/podcasting)

**Title**: I built a free, local-first desktop app for converting sermons & podcasts into 9:16 vertical shorts (no subscriptions, no minutes limits)

> Hey everyone,
> 
> Like many creators and church media volunteers, I was tired of:
> 1. Cloud-based clipping tools charging $30-$90/mo with monthly processing caps and watermarks.
> 2. Manual keyframing in Premiere/Resolve taking 3-5 hours just to produce 5 short clips.
> 
> So we built **hclipper**—a standalone desktop app (.exe) powered by Electron, FFmpeg hardware acceleration, and local OpenCV face tracking.
> 
> **Key Capabilities:**
> - **Direct URL Ingestion**: Paste a YouTube/Facebook URL or drop an MP4.
> - **Visual Timeline Scrubber**: Interactive WaveSurfer waveform player with in/out range markers.
> - **Cadence Trimming**: Removes awkward silence while preserving natural 0.34s speaker breathing rhythm.
> - **Broadcast Audio DSP**: Highpass vocal filter, dynamic compression, background music ducking, and -14 LUFS loudness normalization.
> - **On-Device 9:16 Reframing**: YuNet ONNX face tracking keeps the speaker centered without cloud APIs.
> - **Safe-Zone Captions**: Generates styled ASS animated captions positioned above TikTok/IG UI overlays.
> - **Multi-Account Publisher**: Session-persistent publishing to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels.
> 
> **Download & Code:**
> Everything is 100% open source under MIT:
> GitHub: https://github.com/ea-manni/hclipper
> 
> Download the pre-built Windows installer (`hclipper Studio Setup 1.0.0.exe`) or run from source with Node & Python.
> 
> Feedback, feature ideas, and contributions are super welcome!
